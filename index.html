Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Is your broast 100% Halal?

Yes. All our chicken is sourced from certified halal suppliers and prepared according to halal standards, so you can enjoy every bite with complete peace of mind.

What makes Master Injected Broast special?

Our signature injection technique infuses a 12-spice blend deep into the meat, followed by a 24-hour marination. The result is flavor in every bite, not just on the crust.

Do you offer delivery?

Absolutely. We deliver hot and fresh across the city, typically within 30 minutes. A flat Rs 50 delivery fee applies city wide.

Can I customize the spice level?

Of course! Choose from Classic, Spicy, or our family platter. Mention your preferred spice level in the order notes and we'll tailor it for you.

Do you cater for events?

Yes, we provide bulk broast platters for parties, offices, and gatherings. Reach out via WhatsApp and we'll craft a custom package for your event.
